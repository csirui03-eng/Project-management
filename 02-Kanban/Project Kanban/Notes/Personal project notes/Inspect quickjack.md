# Hydraulic Lift Inspection Checklist (After Storage)

Use this checklist before trusting portable hydraulic lifts that have been sitting idle for extended periods.

## Pre-Use Inspection

### 1. Visual Fluid Check

- [ ] Locate sight glass or reservoir cap
- [ ] Check fluid colour - should be clear amber/reddish
- [ ] Look for milky or cloudy appearance (indicates water contamination)

### 2. Unloaded Cycle Test

- [ ] Extend and retract fully with no load
- [ ] Repeat 3-5 times
- [ ] Listen for cavitation (gravel/crackling sounds)
- [ ] Check for jerky or spongy movement

### 3. Seal Inspection

- [ ] Look for oil residue around cylinder seals
- [ ] Check all fittings for weeping
- [ ] Inspect after cycling - seals may leak once warmed up

### 4. Ram/Cylinder Rod Inspection

- [ ] Check chrome rod surface for pitting
- [ ] Look for rust spots
- [ ] Check for scoring or scratches

## Decision Guide

|Observation|Action|
|---|---|
|Fluid clear, smooth operation, no leaks|Safe to use|
|Milky/cloudy fluid|Drain and refill before use|
|Cavitation noise|Drain and refill before use|
|Seal weepage|Replace seals or retire the lift|
|Pitted/scored ram|Retire the lift - seal damage will follow|

## Fluid Replacement Notes

If fluid needs replacing:

1. Remove fill plug
2. Fully retract the ram
3. Turn unit upside down to drain
4. Allow to drain completely
5. Return upright and refill with fresh hydraulic jack oil
6. Cycle several times to bleed air
7. Top up fluid level and replace plug

## Safety Reminder

Never work under a vehicle supported only by a hydraulic lift. Always use rated jack stands as secondary support once the vehicle is raised.
