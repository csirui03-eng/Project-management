## Where impedance comes from

![[Fahy fig 5.1 single leaf model.jpeg]]

Force plays voltage, velocity plays current, mass is an inductor, a spring is a capacitor, a damper is a resistor.

That dictionary predates the wall problem by decades (AC circuit theory, Heaviside, 1880s), so writing the leaf in velocity terms inherits every theorem circuits already proved: resonance, series addition, power transfer.

In velocity currency the fluid itself collapses to a bare real constant, rho c.

The governing relation is Ohm's law with a wall in it.
