## In one paragraph
Thirty verification test cases, each a small problem with a classical or published reference solution used to check one Mechanical APDL capability. The slice leans towards contact and friction (Hertz contact, squeal damping, wear, interference fit, pipe on ground), fracture (T-stress, C* integral, XFEM crack angle, unstructured-mesh K_I), unbounded domains (PML, infinite elements, Boussinesq), acoustics and vibro-acoustics (visco-thermal transmission loss, muffler with mean flow, room-acoustic diffusion, piston-fluid MSUP), and dynamics (missing mass spectrum, wind-load PSD, rotating plate stress stiffening). Each case states reference, analysis type, element types, geometry, material data, loading, modelling notes, and a target-versus-APDL results table with ratios. The manual is a map of what each element and command combination has been validated against, not a tutorial.

## Key ideas
- FLUID116 pipe flow coupled to a solid via SURF152 film coefficients reproduces the analytical fluid temperature rise along a hollow cylinder; the same problem is repeated with SOLID70, SHELL131, layered SOLID278 and SHELL294 walls (VM271).
- 2D and 3D frictional Hertz contact with surface-projection contact (KEYOPT(4)=3) and stress-based stiffness update (KEYOPT(10)=2); analytic pressure decays to zero faster than FE near the contact edge, attributed to mesh discretisation (VM272).
- Shape memory alloy with thermal effect (TB,SMA): one tension-compression cycle at body temperatures 285.15 K and 253.15 K, phase-transformation stresses compared against Auricchio (VM273).
- Stabilising squeal damping via contact real constant FDMS; CMROTATE imposes sliding, DAMP eigensolver returns the modal damping ratio of a sliding spring-mass block (VM274).
- Brake squeal mode lock-in on a pin-disc model, three procedures (full nonlinear perturbed, partial nonlinear perturbed, linear non-prestressed modal), unsymmetric eigensolver; pin length tuned so disc third bending (2246 Hz) couples with pin second bending (2279 Hz) (VM275).
- Transient moisture diffusion with SOLID239 using normalised concentration; flux chosen so concentration stays below saturation; target from Crank Eq. 4.55 truncated to five terms (VM276).
- Hall plate: offset voltage measured first with no field, then subtracted from the output at B = 0.8 T to give the Hall voltage; circuit source coupled by CP,VOLT (VM277).
- Pipe on rigid ground under gravity with traction-based line contact (KEYOPT(3)=1); penetration follows p/k with k = 1e6 force/length^3 (VM278).
- T-stress at a centre crack via CINT, solved in 2D plane strain and 3D with UZ constrained to enforce plane strain (VM279).
- Doubly insulated steam pipe conduction with convection at both surfaces; solved with SOLID278, SOLID279 and SOLID291; the tetrahedral SOLID291 case hits the target to four figures (VM280).
- Stress stiffening versus spin softening: rotation about X (in-plane axis) engages both effects, rotation about Z leaves spin softening negligible; linear perturbation modal after nonlinear static (VM281).
- Piston-fluid column MSUP harmonic with unsymmetric modal solve, MPC contact between piston and fluid, FSI interface flag, modal damping via MDAMP (VM282).
- Low reduced frequency visco-thermal fluid model: double-wall panel transmission loss 10 to 300 Hz, TL from ratio of average pressures, no explicit load required (VM283).
- Missing mass method in single-point response spectrum analysis: four modes plus missing mass at ZPA 0.5, SRSS combination plus absolute missing-mass response (VM284).
- C* integral for creep crack growth (strain-hardening creep, load held 1000 h), averaged over contours 2 to 9; reference value C* = 0.0319 from Kanninen and Popelar h1 = 8.57 (VM285).
- Archard wear (TB,WEAR) of a block under compression then sliding; wear relieves the applied displacement so pressure and wear rate decay; solved with augmented Lagrangian (2D) and Lagrange multiplier (3D) (VM286).
- XFEM initial crack-growth angle under pure shear, maximum circumferential stress criterion (TB,CGCR with PSMAX; CGROW); angle recovered from the nodal PHI level-set value (VM287).
- Enforced motion method for piezoelectric harmonic response: MODCONT,,ON generates pseudo-static modes, DVAL applies the voltage as enforced displacement, admittance from POST26 (VM288).
- Pressurised spherical cavity in an infinite medium truncated by PML (SOLID186 KEYOPT(15)=1), one-eighth symmetry, checked against the closed-form 1/r^2 displacement field (VM289).
- Same cavity problem solved with INFIN257 semi-infinite elements generated by EINFIN (VM290).
- Boussinesq point load on a half space with finite plus infinite element mesh; the 1/r singularity means near-origin FE values are not expected to match (VM291).
- Interference fit contact pressure with SECTYPE,CONTACT geometry correction against Shigley Eq. 3.57; Poisson ratio set to 0.0 to match the reference (VM292).
- K_I along a penny-shaped crack front using the unstructured mesh method with SOLID285 tetrahedra, one-eighth model (VM293).
- Fourier coefficients of a diode-rectified circuit (CIRCU125 ideal diode) for three capacitor values, transient solve then Fourier decomposition (VM294).
- Terzaghi 1D consolidation with permeability varying linearly with depth (TB,PM), CPT212 coupled pore pressure elements, pore pressure at 6 m depth over 0.1 to 0.5 day (VM295).
- Thermoplastic cylinder expansion: coupled-field PLANE222/223, bilinear isotropic hardening with thermal softening, 0.001 s load duration approximates the adiabatic limit; Taylor-Quinney factor 0.9 (VM296).
- Muffler acoustics with uniform mean flow at Mach 0.3, FLUID220, Robin radiation outlet, distributed solve with DDOPTION,FREQ (VM297).
- 40-storey building under partly correlated Davenport wind spectrum: PSD analysis with COMBIN14/MASS21 stick model, response PSD converted to 2-sided m^2/rad/s to match the reference (VM298).
- Room-acoustic diffusion model with atmospheric attenuation: steady-state FLUID220 solve gives SPL along a line in a flat room (VM299).
- Sound transmission between two rooms through a partition wall using the diffusion model; SPL difference checked against statistical room acoustics (VM300).

## Equations that matter
$$\mathrm{SPL} = 10\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{\rho\, c_0^2\, w}{P_{\mathrm{ref}}^2}\right)$$
Sound pressure level from diffuse sound energy density $w$, $P_{\mathrm{ref}} = 2\times10^{-5}$ (VM299, VM300). Unnumbered in the source.

$$\mathrm{SPL}_1 - \mathrm{SPL}_2 = R - 10\log_{10}(S_{12}/A_{22}), \qquad A_{22} = \alpha S_2 + \tau S_{12}, \qquad \tau = 10^{-R/10}$$
Statistical-theory SPL difference between coupled rooms; the target for the diffusion-model comparison (VM300). Unnumbered in the source.

$$M_t = M_0 + t\,w\,h, \qquad M_0 = 2\,l\,h\,w\,C_0 C_{\mathrm{sat}}$$
Total moisture weight gain of the plate; $M_0$ is the initial moisture weight at $t = 0$ s (VM276). Unnumbered in the source.

Eq. (274-1), the damped frequency from friction, is present only as an embedded image; its stated evaluation is $\lambda_{1,2} = 0.003125 \pm 6.274949\mathrm{i}$ rad/s, giving damping ratio $4.980\times10^{-4}$ (VM274).

Most other governing and reference equations in this build are embedded SVG images and are identified here by case, not transcribed: fluid temperature rise (VM271), Crank Eq. 4.55 (VM276), Hall voltage Eq. 9.14 (VM277), C* reference formula (VM285), Archard wear rate (VM286), XFEM angle from PHI (VM287), cavity displacement and stress fields (VM289, VM290), Boussinesq vertical displacement (VM291), Shigley Eq. 3.57 (VM292), Fourier series (VM294).

## Numbers worth citing
- Worst contact friction ratio 1.129 at x = 0.301 mm in the 2D Hertz case; 2D contact pressure ratio reaches 1.057 at x = 0.601 mm, near the contact edge (VM272).
- Squeal unstable mode: target 2215 Hz, APDL 2259 to 2270 Hz depending on procedure, ratios 0.976 to 0.980 (VM275).
- Interface temperature at x = 0.1675 m: target 48.03 degC, SOLID278 gives 50.07 degC (ratio 1.042), SOLID279 50.13 degC (1.044); SOLID291 gives 48.03 degC (1.000) (VM280).
- MSUP piston-fluid quasi-static response ratios 1.052 (displacement) and 1.056 (pressure) at 1.0 Hz; at resonance f1 both are 0.998 (VM282).
- Transmission loss ratio drops to 0.908 at 286 Hz and 0.921 at 300 Hz, top of the 10 to 300 Hz range (VM283).
- Spectrum accelerations within 5% of reference at all eleven elevations, worst ratio 1.05 (VM284).
- C* integral 0.033 versus target 0.032, ratio 1.041, load held 1000 h at 150 MPa (VM285).
- XFEM initial crack-growth angle 66.0 deg versus analytical 70.5 deg, ratio 0.936, the weakest result in the slice (VM287).
- Admittance interpolated from the reference graph: ratios span 0.917 (50 kHz) to 1.080 (35 kHz) (VM288).
- PML radial stress at the cavity wall: -736.7 Pa versus -750 Pa applied, ratio 0.982 (VM289).
- Boussinesq UY below the load: PLANE182 ratios to 0.949, PLANE183 first point 0.920; singularity at the load point stated as the cause (VM291).
- PLANE223 maximum temperature variation 62.67 K versus target 58.9 K, ratio 1.064, adiabatic-limit case (VM296).
- Muffler peak resonance 2950 Hz versus 3000 Hz target with 50 Hz frequency steps (100 substeps over 0 to 5000 Hz) (VM297).
- Wind PSD standard deviation of top-floor displacement 4.197e-2 m versus 4.65e-2 m, ratio 0.903; first frequency ratio 1.06 (VM298).
- Coupled-room SPL difference 28.59 dB versus 27.00 dB, ratio 1.059 (VM300).

## Definitions introduced
- Offset voltage - output voltage of the Hall plate with no magnetic field, caused here by non-uniform meshing making the current distribution asymmetric (VM277).
- Missing mass response - residual term added to the mode combination to account for modes above those extracted, summed absolutely with the SRSS modal response (VM284).
- PHI value - signed normal distance of a node from the crack in the XFEM level-set description, used to recover the crack-growth angle (VM287).
- Enforced motion (pseudo-static) modes - modes generated with MODCONT,,ON at supports flagged by D with a base identification number, enabling base excitation in MSUP analyses (VM288).
- Stabilising squeal damping - contact damping activated through real constant FDMS to stabilise sliding contact in squeal analyses (VM274).
- Taylor-Quinney factor - fraction of plastic work converted to heat in the coupled thermomechanical model, 0.9 here (VM296).

## Flags
- Every case has a companion file Hlp_V_VM<N>TXT.html holding the full APDL input listing (vmNNN.dat); not read in this pass, per instruction.
- VM288 explicitly cross-references VM176 (outside this slice) for modelling notes and reuses its problem sketch graphic; the indexer should stitch VM288 to VM176.
- VM285, VM290 and VM293 require supplemental .cdb mesh files from the VM2024R2_MAPDL.zip download; the .dat listing alone does not run.
- Suspected erratum in VM297: the results table labels the pressure amplitude row "frequency=20,000Hz" while the sweep is 0 to 5000 Hz and the surrounding text quotes 2000 Hz.
- VM294 lists ratio 0.000 for the A1 coefficient at Tau = 0 (target 0.000, APDL 0.0151); ratio undefined for a zero target.
- VM271 modelling notes say the problem is "solved for four cases" then "static solve is performed for all three cases"; the results table has four cases.
- Nearly all equations in this HTML build are embedded SVG images; equation content could not be transcribed except where repeated in body text (noted per case above).
- Minor source typos observed: "sovled" (VM293), "instal loading" (VM285), reference title misspellings in VM273.
